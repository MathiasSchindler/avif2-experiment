## Interpretation and limitations

- Non-DCT transforms, directional deltas, MRL, multi-line MRL, CfL, FSC, and IntraBC have the broadest corpus demand.
- Palette appears only in `silene-384x307-qp080.obu`. Transform skip appears only 14 times across seven files.
- The census schema reports no nonzero secondary-transform occurrence, while production parsing exposes seven live IST tuples. The focused reconstruction matrix and artifact walks provide the current IST evidence.
- `enable_ibp` records sequence capability. AVM applies IBP deterministically to eligible transform sizes; it is not a separately signaled per-block usage flag.
- Support classifications are a reviewed snapshot in `tools/census/support.json`. Update that manifest when decoder evidence changes; the generator validates coverage but cannot prove the entries.
- The not-fully-supported ranking measures feature demand only. It does not include coefficient-profile or traversal blockers because those dimensions are outside the current AVM census schema.
- AVM was built with `CONFIG_ACCOUNTING=0`. Entropy symbols, CDF contexts, EOB values, and coefficient levels are outside this census.
