# AV2 smooth and Paeth incremental intra model

Status: the independent model is complete and its four luma modes are admitted
behind explicit production `--search intra`. Chroma-mode search remains
modeled only.

## Syntax and tool interaction

Smooth, Smooth-V, Smooth-H, and Paeth are the five non-directional luma
entries at `y_mode_index` 1, 2, 3, and 4 after DC at 0. The
`y_mode_index` CDF context remains the number (0..2) of directional left and
above joint modes. `y_mode_set=0` and, when allowed, `fsc_mode=0` are coded.
MRLS syntax is absent because these four modes are non-directional.

Chroma first codes `is_cfl=0` when CfL or MHCCP makes that decision present,
then `uv_mode` with context `is_directional_mode(YMode)`. With non-directional
luma, DC/Smooth/Smooth-V/Smooth-H/Paeth/V/H are symbols 0..6. With
directional luma, the colocated luma mode is symbol 0, followed by DC,
Smooth, Smooth-V, Smooth-H, and Paeth. Smooth and Paeth have no sequence or
frame bitstream enable flag; AVM's `enable-smooth-intra` and
`enable-paeth-intra` are encoder search controls, both defaulting to one.

The model fixes FSC, CfL, DIP, and IBP off for each candidate. FSC affects
coefficient interpretation, not the predictor, and remains explicitly coded
as zero. MRLS applies only to directional luma. CfL replaces chroma intra
mode prediction when selected, so the independent smooth/Paeth chroma trials
must code `is_cfl=0`.

## Predictors and boundaries

Smooth uses the normative two linear corner interpolants plus the power-of-two
near-edge blend. `scale = Round2(log2W + log2H - 4, 2)`;
`sTop = 32 >> min(6, (row*2)>>scale)` and likewise for `sLeft`. Smooth-V
returns the vertical result, Smooth-H the horizontal result, and Smooth rounds
their sum by one bit. The bottom-left and top-right endpoints are `LeftCol[h]` and `AboveRow[w]`
when those reconstructed extension samples are available, otherwise the final
in-block edge samples are repeated.

Paeth computes `base = top + left - top_left`, then chooses the nearest of
left, top, and top-left, resolving ties in that order. Short available edges
repeat their final reconstructed sample. With neither edge, AboveRow is 127,
LeftCol is 129, and the corner is 128. With one edge, the missing edge and
corner use the first sample of the available edge.

The admitted kernel matrix is 4x4, 4x8, 8x4, 8x8, 8x16, 16x8, and 16x16,
covering I420 chroma and I400/I444 luma/chroma generated by the fixed leaves.

## Frozen evidence and gate

`tests/smooth_paeth_vectors.json` freezes 224 cases across I400/I420/I444:
flat and horizontal/vertical gradients, corners, quadrant edges, synthetic
text/detail, and hash-identified prepared pitti-ceiling crops. It also freezes
16 missing/short-edge cases, exact syntax tell transitions, and 28 kernel
hashes (four modes over seven sizes).

The local model, pinned AVM high-bit-depth C kernels, and project decoder
kernel each pass all 28 predictor comparisons: **84/84 parity checks**.
Candidate rate is the exact delta from a cloned range writer and adaptive CDF
state; losing trials cannot mutate the committed state. Stable ties retain
DC, V, H, Smooth, Smooth-V, Smooth-H, then Paeth.

Against DC/V/H, the whole 224-case model holds estimated syntax at 108 bytes
and reduces aggregate predictor SSE from 82,861,817 to 76,832,967 (7.28%),
with 112 incremental wins. The locked 112-case corner/edge/text/detail subset
reduces SSE from 80,044,960 to 74,053,386 (7.49%) with 60 wins, while isolated
mode syntax rises from 50 to 52 bytes. This passes the model admission gate:
whole-corpus bytes do not increase, whole and targeted SSE improve by at least
5%, and at least one quarter of cases select a new mode. It does not claim
complete-stream encoder savings; production integration remains a later task.

The full Python model, including prepared-crop loading and all transactional
candidate evaluations, runs in under one second on the recorded host. See
`metrics/smooth-paeth-model.json` for the locked counts and observed runtime.

## Production admission

The production surface trials DC, V, H, Smooth, Smooth-V, Smooth-H, then
Paeth with stable ties and fresh complete-stream state. Smooth-family trials
cover every admitted I400/I420/I444 image dimension from 1 through 1440.
The fixed superblock traversal tracks reconstructed 4x4 cells separately per
plane; optional top-right and bottom-left endpoints are used only when the
decoder would expose them. FSC is zero, MRLS is zero only for V/H, CfL is
zero, and chroma uses DC prediction.

The locked 224-case full-stream subset passes exact local reconstruction for
every selected Smooth-family mode and exact pinned-AVM/project parity for
every stream: 5,319 versus 5,326 bytes, Y SSE 43,518,512 versus 43,744,028,
unchanged U/V SSE, and 38 incremental wins (Smooth 13, Smooth-V 21, Smooth-H
2, Paeth 2).

`metrics/smooth-paeth-full-gate.json` adds three synthetic I400/I420/I444
1440x1440 cases and one prepared 1440x1440 I400 image. All four are
deterministic with exact local/AVM/project reconstruction. Smooth wins two
synthetic color cases, reducing aggregate SSE by 1,031,790 and aggregate size
by 410 bytes; the I400 and prepared cases do not regress. Median/worst
`--search intra` time is 8.59/23.24 seconds and peak workspace is 18,760,368
bytes.
